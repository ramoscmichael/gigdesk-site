1.Overview

Balloon Pop is built to work entirely offline: there is no account to create, no sign‑in, and no server that the app talks to for gameplay. Your level progress, high scores, and sound settings live only on your own device.

The one exception is advertising: Balloon Pop shows ads through Google's AdMob service to support free play, and Google's ad SDK does communicate over the network on its own. Section 3 below explains exactly what that involves.

2.Data stored on your device

Balloon Pop saves a small amount of gameplay data locally on your device, using Android's standard app storage (SharedPreferences). None of it is transmitted anywhere by us, and none of it identifies you personally.

Stored valueWhat it's for

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data. We never see it, and we don't collect names, emails, photos, contacts, or location.

3.Advertising (Google AdMob)

Balloon Pop shows two kinds of ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob):

  • An occasional interstitial ad after finishing a level (skippable).
  • An optional rewarded ad if you choose to "Continue" after running out of time — this one is only shown if you tap the Continue button yourself.

To select and measure these ads, Google's SDK may collect information such as your advertising ID, general device information, approximate location (from IP address), and ad interaction data, and may use it for ad personalization and frequency capping, subject to your device's ad privacy settings. This app does not receive or store any of that data itself — it's handled entirely by Google.

You can review or reset how ads are personalized for you at any time in your device's own settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads on most Android phones), and read Google's own disclosures at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and AdMob's data disclosure.

4.App permissions

Balloon Pop requests exactly one Android permission:

  • Internet access — used solely so the ads described above can load. Gameplay itself needs no network connection at all.

The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

5.Children's privacy

Balloon Pop is a general‑audience casual game and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it — though in practice, since the app collects no personal information at all, there should be nothing to remove beyond locally stored game progress on that device.

6.Data security

Because your gameplay data never leaves your device, it's protected by the same security as the rest of your phone (lock screen, disk encryption, etc. — all managed by your device's operating system, not by us). We don't operate any servers or databases that could be breached, because we don't have any.

7.Your choices

  • Turn sound effects or music on/off any time from the in‑app Settings screen.
  • Reset your device's advertising ID or opt out of interest‑based ads from your device's system settings.
  • Uninstall the app at any time to remove all locally stored data immediately.

8.Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example if a future update adds a new feature that touches data differently — we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

9.Contact us

Questions about this policy or how Balloon Pop handles data? Reach out at theramosestech@gmail.com.
